ABOUT THE LABEL

A catalogue built around listening.

ArtBeat Music is a Russian independent label founded in 2010, focused on contemporary improvisational music and neighbouring forms.

The catalogue includes jazz as its core, alongside funk, jazz-rock, new age, progressive and art-rock, world music and chamber improvisation.

This store presents selected physical releases and information for listeners, collectors and musicians looking to order from abroad.

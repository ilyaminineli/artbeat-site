ABOUT ARTBEAT

A Russian independent
music label.

ArtBeat Music was founded in 2010 and is associated with contemporary improvisational music, with jazz at the centre of its catalogue.

The catalogue reaches into neighbouring forms including jazz-rock, funk, progressive and art-rock, world music, chamber music and other experimental practices.

The international version of the store is designed for listeners, collectors, musicians and curators who want to discover the label and enquire about physical releases.
